Curriculum

Eight weeks that take you from the alphabet to writing and presenting your own stories.

  1. Week1

    • Letters
    • SPOK concept (Subject–Predicate–Object–Complement)
    • Present tense
    • Examination of the Subject (S) concept (formal and informal usage)

  2. Week2

    • Examination of the Predicate (P) concept (verbs and adjectives)
    • Prepositions
    • Examination of the Object (O) concept

  3. Week3

    • Prepositions (continued)
    • Past tense

  4. Week4

    • Future tense
    • Demonstratives
    • Possessive suffixes

  5. Week5

    • Conversation practice

  6. Week6

    • Listening practice
    • Conjunctions

  7. Week7

    • Writing short stories and presenting them

  8. Week8

    • Integrated development of reading, writing, speaking, and listening skills
